Privacy

What this site does — and, more importantly, what it doesn’t do — with your data.

The short version

No analytics. No advertising. No tracking cookies.

  • This site does not collect, store, or share personal data on its own.
  • No analytics scripts, ad networks, or third-party trackers are loaded.
  • No cookies are set by this site. The sidebar open/closed preference is saved in your browser’s localStorage and never leaves your device.

Hosting & infrastructure

Where the bytes actually come from

The site is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare as a reverse proxy and CDN. As part of operating those services, GitHub and Cloudflare may automatically log technical request data — such as IP address, user-agent, country, and request timestamps — for security, abuse mitigation, and traffic delivery.

That data is governed by their respective policies:

GitHub Privacy Statement Cloudflare Privacy Policy

Third-party assets

Fonts and icons loaded from public CDNs

The site fetches the Inter typeface from Google Fonts and the Boxicons icon set from the unpkg CDN. When your browser requests those assets, the respective providers receive standard request metadata (IP, user-agent, referrer). This site adds no identifiers to those requests.

Contact form

How the message reaches me

The contact form opens your default email client via a mailto: link. No data is submitted to this site or any server — it goes directly from your email client to mine.
